Estimation frameworks & lessons
01 / Approach

Top-Down

  • Start with population
  • Filter by geography, income, age
  • Apply penetration rates
  • Calculate frequency + value
02 / Approach

Bottom-Up

  • Start with known data points
  • Build up from cities/companies
  • Scale to total market
  • Triangulate with top-down
03 / Anchors

India Anchors

  • Population: 1.4B
  • Urban: 35% = 500M
  • Internet users: 850M+
  • GDP per capita: $2,400

Interview preparation checklist
01

Day before — Light review

Review your key frameworks. Don't over-study. Rest well. Visualize yourself solving a case clearly and confidently.

02

Morning of — Warm-up

Do 2–3 mini guesstimates (5 mins each) to get in the zone. Eat well. Arrive 15 mins early.

03

In the interview — Mindset

Remember: the interviewer WANTS you to succeed. They're looking for structure + thinking, not perfect answers. Breathe. Think out loud. Ask questions.

04

Speak to listen

Watch the interviewer's cues. If they nod → move on. If confused → clarify. If interested in a direction → dig deeper there.

Daily consulting trivia
Today's Question
What does MECE stand for and why is it important in consulting?
Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive. It's important because it ensures your problem breakdown has no overlap and covers all possibilities. This prevents analytical gaps and makes your recommendations stronger.
